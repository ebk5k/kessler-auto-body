Insurance

No. In California you have the legal right to choose your repair shop. We work directly with every major insurer, handle the paperwork and supplements, and keep you copied on everything so nothing happens to your car without you knowing.

Your deductible is paid to us at pickup, and it's the only part of an insured repair you pay directly. We'll tell you the exact number before work starts, and we'll never quietly inflate an estimate to "absorb" it — that's insurance fraud, and it's how repairs get cut short.

We document why OEM is required — fit, safety systems, and paint adhesion — and negotiate it with the adjuster on your behalf. Where a policy strictly excludes OEM, we show you the difference in cost so you can decide, not the insurer.

Repair process

Most cosmetic repairs take 3–5 business days. Collision work with structural repair typically runs 8–14 days, depending on parts availability. You'll get a written timeline with the estimate and photo updates along the way — and a call the moment anything changes.

Yes. If your policy includes rental coverage we arrange it and have the car waiting when you drop off. If it doesn't, we have negotiated day rates with two rental partners a block from the shop.

Around the clock. Call or text the shop number and we dispatch a flatbed anywhere in the San Fernando Valley — the tow is folded into the insured repair whenever the claim allows.

Paint & warranty

Yes. The factory paint code is only the starting point — every car fades differently. We read your car's actual color with a spectrophotometer, spray test cards, and check them under daylight and shop light before painting. Repairs are blended into adjacent panels so there's no visible edge.

Our workmanship and refinish for as long as you own the vehicle: peeling, fading, cracking, and any defect in the repair itself. It's written, it's transferable to no one, and it doesn't expire.
